About this certification

TRE™ is a body-led series of simple, powerful exercises that release stress, tension and trauma and build resilience — safely activating the body’s natural tremor reflex so the nervous system returns to balance. The Global TRE™ Provider Certification qualifies you to offer TRE™ to the general public, in both individual and group settings.

The 2027 Singapore cohort is co-taught by two TRE™ Trainers — Isabelle Claus Teixeira and Simba Stenqvist — for richer, varied learning. Only one cohort runs in Singapore each year, so register your interest early to secure your place.

Three in-person modules in Singapore, each paired with online TRE™ sessions and supervisions.

Module 127–28 Feb 2027 · Singapore

A 2-day in-person workshop plus personal sessions: 2 online individual sessions (led by a different TRE™ trainer) and 2 online group sessions (1 Apr & 1 Jun 2027, 7–9pm SGT).

Module 23–4 Jul 2027 · Singapore

Teaching TRE™ to One Person

A 2-day in-person workshop plus 2 online group supervisions (19 Jul & 16 Aug 2027) and 2 online individual supervisions with a different trainer — building your skills to guide one person safely through TRE™.

A 2-day in-person workshop plus 3 online individual supervisions with a different trainer and 1 online group supervision (13 Nov 2027). The final module: complete it, its supervisions and the remaining certification requirements to become a Global TRE™ Provider.

Joining a session online? Prepare your space

Your trainer works with you through the camera, so we must see you head to toe, both standing and on the mat. A quick setup before the session makes all the difference.

  • Device about 2–2.5 m away, landscape, roughly hip height — to the side of your mat
  • A yoga mat, good lighting, and loose, light-coloured clothing (dark hides your tremors)
  • Reliable internet and the Zoom app, joined a few minutes early
