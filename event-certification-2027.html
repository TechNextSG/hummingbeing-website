The Certification

Become a Certified
TRE™ Provider

TRE™ is a body-led series of simple, powerful exercises to release stress, tension and trauma and build resilience. It safely activates the body’s natural reflex of shaking or vibrating, calming the nervous system so the body returns to a balanced state.

The Global TRE™ Provider Certification allows certificate holders to offer TRE™ to the general public in both individual and group settings. The 2027 Singapore cohort is boosted by Internal Alchemy and taught by two TRE™ Trainers.

Only one cohort per year runs in Singapore — register your interest early to secure your place.

What makes this cohort special:

  • Taught by two TRE™ Trainers for richer, varied learning
  • ICF CCE for coaches with Module 1
  • Internal Alchemy workshops to upgrade your skills once certified
  • A vibrant community of practitioners learning together
The Journey

Your 2027 Journey

February to October 2027 — Singapore & Bali. Three modules plus From Shaking to Shaping and two Internal Alchemy intro days. One cohort per year.

Module 1 27–28 Feb 2027 Singapore · Personal Practice

Your Personal TRE™ Practice

For your personal use — can be purchased alone. Includes the 2-day workshop and 4 online TRE™ sessions: 2 personal & individual sessions (led by a different TRE™ trainer) and 2 experienced in a group setting. ICF CCE for coaches included.

Module 2 2–3 Jul 2027 Singapore · Teaching to 1 Person

Teaching TRE™ to One Person

Open after completing Module 1. A 2-day workshop plus supervisions (individual & personal with a different trainer, and in a group), building your skills to guide one person safely through TRE™.

Workshop 4 Jul 2027 Singapore · 1 Day

From Shaking to Shaping

A one-day workshop exploring the use of TRE™ in a coaching context — how neurogenic tremoring supports embodied presence, self-care and client work.

Internal Alchemy 28 Oct 2027 Bali or Singapore · 1 Day

Intro to Internal Alchemy — Lead One

A one-day introduction to Internal Alchemy for working with one person — an integrated system of breathwork, fascial release, grounding and tremor work, created by Simba Stenqvist.

Module 3 29–30 Oct 2027 Bali or Singapore · Teaching to Groups

Teaching TRE™ to Groups

Open after completing Module 2. A 2-day TRE™ workshop plus supervisions (individual & personal with a different trainer, and in a group). Completes your Global TRE™ Provider certification.

Internal Alchemy 31 Oct 2027 Bali or Singapore · 1 Day

Intro to Internal Alchemy — Work with a Group

A one-day introduction to Internal Alchemy for working with groups — upgrade your skills to bring the integrated system into group settings.
