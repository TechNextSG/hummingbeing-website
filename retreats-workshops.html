Why a Retreat?

Deep Healing Needs Space

1:1 sessions are powerful — but retreats and workshops create something different. When a group of people commit to a shared experience together, the collective field amplifies the individual journey. There is permission to go deeper, let go more fully and be witnessed in the process.

HummingBeing retreats and workshops are carefully designed immersive experiences — combining TRE™, Somatic Healing, Breathwork, movement and reflection — in spaces that invite genuine transformation.

Our Offerings

Types of Retreats & Workshops

From half-day introductions to multi-day immersions — in Singapore, Japan and beyond.

Half-Day & Day Workshops

Focused, accessible group sessions introducing somatic practices — TRE™, Breathwork or EFT — in a supportive group setting. Perfect for those curious about somatic work or wanting a deeper dive on a specific modality.

Weekend Retreats

Multi-day immersions in carefully chosen locations in Singapore and Japan. A combination of group somatic practice, individual integration time, nature connection and nourishing meals — designed to reset the nervous system at depth.

Corporate Wellness

Bespoke somatic wellness sessions designed for teams — addressing stress, burnout, communication and resilience at the organisational level. Delivered in-house or off-site, half-day to full-day formats.

TRE™ Group Sessions

Facilitated group TRE™ practice — experiencing the body’s natural tremor mechanism together in community. A powerful introduction to TRE™ and an effective regular practice for existing practitioners.

Somatic Healing Circles

Intimate group healing experiences combining Reiki, Breathwork, EFT and somatic movement. A safe container for deep rest, release and reconnection. Limited to small groups to ensure depth and personal attention.

Professional Training

Workshops and training programmes for coaches, therapists and wellness practitioners wanting to deepen their embodied practice and integrate somatic approaches into their work with clients.

What to Expect

A Typical Retreat Experience

Each retreat is unique — but every one follows the same arc of arrival, release and integration.

  1. 1

    Arrival & Grounding

    We begin by slowing down — settling into the body, getting present in the space, and meeting the group with curiosity and openness. No performance, no agenda imposed from the outside.

  2. 2

    Somatic Practice

    Guided TRE™, Breathwork, movement or Somatic Healing sessions — supported by Isabelle throughout. The pace follows the group’s natural rhythm, not a clock.

  3. 3

    Rest & Integration

    Space to rest, journal, reflect or simply be. The body needs time to integrate what has been released — this is not downtime, it is an essential part of the healing arc.

  4. 4

    Sharing & Closing

    Optional group sharing in a confidential, supportive container. A closing practice to anchor the experience and prepare you to carry it into daily life.
