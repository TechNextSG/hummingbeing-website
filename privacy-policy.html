Privacy Policy

How HummingBeing collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who We Are

HummingBeing is a somatic wellness practice operated by Isabelle Claus Teixeira, part of BHD Asia & BHD Japan. We offer TRE®, Somatic Coaching and Somatic Bodywork services in Singapore, Japan and online globally.

Contact: hello@hummingbeing.com  ·  WhatsApp

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill in our contact or booking enquiry form
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or event notifications
  • Contact us directly by email or WhatsApp

This may include your name, email address, phone number, location and details about your wellness goals.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and arrange discovery calls or sessions
  • Send you information about upcoming events or workshops (only if you opted in)
  • Improve our services and website experience

We will never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

Enquiry form submissions are processed via FormSubmit and delivered directly to our email. We take reasonable precautions to protect your data, though no internet transmission is completely secure.

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or maintain an active client relationship.

5. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or any third-party tracking technology.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@hummingbeing.com.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external sites (YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

  • Email: hello@hummingbeing.com
  • WhatsApp: +81 806515 1778

Questions? Just Ask.

We're happy to explain how we handle your data or answer any questions you have.
