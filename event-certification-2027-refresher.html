What you attend

You take part in all three in-person modules in Singapore. Supervised options add half of the supervisions, with no personal sessions.

Module 124–25 Apr 2027 · Singapore

Your Personal TRE™ Practice

A 2-day in-person workshop. Personal sessions are not part of the refresher options.

Module 23–4 Jul 2027 · Singapore

Teaching TRE™ to One Person

A 2-day in-person workshop. Supervised options add 1 online group supervision and 1 online individual supervision.

Module 330–31 Oct 2027 · Singapore

Teaching TRE™ to Groups

A 2-day in-person workshop. Supervised options add 1 online individual supervision and 1 online group supervision.
