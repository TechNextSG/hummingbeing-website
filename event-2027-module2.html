About this module

Module 2 turns your personal practice outward. You learn to assess, guide and contain another person through a full TRE™ session — reading the body, pacing the tremor, and knowing when to slow things down.

A 2-day workshop plus supervisions: individual and personal with a different trainer, and in a group setting, so you build your skill with more than one pair of eyes on your work.

Prerequisite: Open after completing Module 1.

Who it is for

  • Graduates of Module 1 continuing the certification
  • Coaches and therapists adding TRE™ to 1:1 work

What is included

  • 2-day in-person workshop in Singapore
  • Individual supervision with a second trainer
  • Group supervision sessions
  • Training manual and materials
