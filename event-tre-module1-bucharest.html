Why TRE™ Module 1 in Bucharest?

This is the first ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1 in Europe — the only TRE™ training anywhere in the world that carries ICF CCEUs (21 CCEUs: 16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies). TRE™, developed by Dr. David Berceli, activates the body’s natural neurogenic tremoring mechanism — a reflex that releases stored stress and trauma, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation.

Completing Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ for personal practice. Combined with Modules 2 and 3, you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider — accredited to offer TRE™ to individuals and groups.

Who it is for

  • A coach (in training or ACC, PCC, MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • A therapist, psychologist or healthcare professional expanding a trauma-informed practice
  • A yoga teacher, bodyworker or somatic practitioner adding neuroscience-backed tools
  • Anyone experiencing chronic stress or burnout and ready for a body-led approach
  • Those considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification (Modules 1, 2 & 3)

Programme — 3 days in Bucharest

Day 115 Oct · 17:00–19:00 EET

Introductory Evening: Self-Care & Presence for Coaches

An introductory evening on nervous system regulation and self-induced therapeutic tremoring — TRE™ as a personal resilience and self-care practice. Counts as the “From Shaking to Shaping” workshop when combined with Module 1 registration.

Day 216 Oct · 09:00–18:00 EET

TRE™ Module 1 — Full Certification Day

The neuroscience of neurogenic tremoring, the TRE™ exercise sequence, somatic awareness, containment and grounding. Guided full TRE™ sessions with direct feedback from your trainers.

Day 317 Oct · 09:00–18:00 EET

TRE™ Module 1 — Continued & Completion

Deepen your practice, integrate the learning and complete Module 1. Q&A, personal practice time and guidance toward Modules 2 and 3. Certificate of completion issued on completion.
