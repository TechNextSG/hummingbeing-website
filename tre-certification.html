About TRE®

Why TRE® Is Worth Certifying In

Evidence-Based

TRE® is backed by over 40 years of research and clinical application across 100+ countries. It has been used with PTSD, trauma, chronic stress and general wellness populations.

Teachable

Unlike many modalities, TRE® can be taught to clients for independent practice — making it a high-value, scalable addition to any practitioner’s toolkit.

Physiologically Grounded

Works directly with the nervous system through the body’s natural tremor mechanism. No need to narrate or revisit traumatic content.

Versatile

Applicable in 1:1 coaching, group facilitation, corporate wellness, trauma support, athletic recovery, personal development and more.

Globally Recognised

TRE® certification is internationally recognised through TRE® for All. Certified Providers join a global network of practitioners.

Fast to See Results

Clients typically experience tangible shifts within 3–4 sessions. The return on investment — for practitioners and clients — is unusually fast.

The Certification Path

How the Certification Works

TRE® Provider certification follows a structured, internationally recognised pathway.

1

Module 1 — In-Person

Foundational TRE® training. Learning the 7 exercises, facilitation basics, understanding the neurophysiology of tremors. This module qualifies you to teach TRE® under supervision.

2

Supervised Practice

A period of supervised client work (minimum required hours) — applying what you have learned with real clients under Isabelle’s guidance.

3

Module 2 — In-Person or Online

Advanced facilitation: working with populations, contraindications, group facilitation, trauma-sensitive practice.

4

Certification

Completing the required supervised hours and demonstrating competency. You receive your Global TRE® Provider certificate from TRE® for All.
