About TRE™

The Benefits of TRE™

TRE™ is a body-led series of simple, powerful exercises to release stress, tension and trauma. It safely activates the body’s natural reflex of shaking or vibrating — calming the nervous system and releasing muscular tension. When this process occurs in a safe and controlled environment, the body naturally returns to a balanced state.

Reported benefits of regular TRE™ practice:

  • Deeper and more restful sleep
  • Increased personal energy for work and home
  • Increased focus, concentration and clearer thinking
  • Greater calm and emotional composure in stressful situations
  • Less worry, anxiety and depression
  • Relief from stress-related physical pain, with increased flexibility and mobility

Learn more about TRE™ worldwide at treglobal.org

The Certification Path

How the Certification Works

Three modules — each a 2-day workshop plus 4 online sessions — take you from personal practice to Certified Global TRE™ Provider.

1

Module 1 — Your Personal TRE™ Practice

2-day workshop + 4 online personal TRE™ sessions. Explore the stress response (anatomy, neurology and physiology), understand the therapeutic tremor response, and experience TRE™ as a self-practice — including self-regulation, containment and grounding strategies. The first step for anyone keen to pursue the certification path.

2

Module 2 — Teaching TRE™ to One Person

2-day workshop + 4 online supervision sessions. Open to those who have completed Module 1 and their personal TRE™ sessions. Deepens Module 1 content, covers Polyvagal theory and basic TRE™ interventions, with hands-on practice teaching TRE™ one-to-one.

3

Module 3 — Teaching TRE™ to Groups

2-day workshop + 4 online supervision sessions. Open to those who have completed all learning requirements from Module 2. Deepens Module 2 content and covers the role of fascia, advanced TRE™ interventions and modifications, transference and counter-transference, and experience leading groups of 3+ persons.

4

Global TRE™ Certified Provider

On completing all three modules you receive your Global TRE™ Provider certificate — qualified to offer TRE™ to the general public in both individual and group settings, and to competently guide clients through the TRE™ process.
