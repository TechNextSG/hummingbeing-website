Why ICF accredited TRE™ Module 1 ONLINE with TRE Scotland?

This is an ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1 — the only TRE™ training anywhere in the world that carries ICF CCEUs (21 CCEUs: 16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies). TRE™, developed by Dr. David Berceli, activates the body’s natural neurogenic tremoring mechanism — a reflex that releases stored stress and trauma, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation.

Completing Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ for personal practice. Combined with Modules 2 and 3, you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider — accredited to offer TRE™ to individuals and groups.

Who it is for

  • Coaches (in training or ACC, PCC, MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • Therapists, psychologists or healthcare professionals expanding a trauma-informed practice
  • Yoga teachers, Pilates teachers, sound healers, breathwork facilitators, bodyworkers or somatic practitioners adding neuroscience-backed tools
  • Anyone experiencing chronic stress or burnout and ready for a body-led approach
  • Those considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification (Modules 1, 2 & 3)

Programme — 2.5 days ONLINE

Day 118 June · 14:00–17:00

Self-Care & Presence for Coaches

An introductory afternoon on nervous system regulation and self-induced therapeutic tremoring — TRE™ as a personal resilience and self-care practice.

Day 219 June · 09:30–18:00

TRE™ Module 1 — Part 1

Day 320 June · 09:30–17:30

TRE™ Module 1 — Part 2 & Completion

Deepen your practice, integrate the learning and complete Module 1. Q&A, personal practice time and guidance toward Modules 2 and 3. ICF CCEs certificate of completion issued on completion and with full attendance.
