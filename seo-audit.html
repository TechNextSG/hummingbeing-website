Global SEO Analysis

Critical Gaps
Critical — No Canonical Tags (all 10 pages) Not a single page has a <link rel="canonical"> tag. Without canonicals, if the site is ever served at multiple URLs (www vs non-www, HTTP vs HTTPS, trailing slash vs no slash), Google may split PageRank across duplicates and index may be unreliable.
Critical — No Open Graph Tags (all 10 pages) When any HummingBeing link is shared on LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp, iMessage, or Slack, it renders as a bare URL with no image, no headline, no description. For a wellness brand whose target audience heavily uses LinkedIn and WhatsApp, this is a significant brand exposure gap. Every page needs og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630px), og:url, og:type. The og:image should be a professional photo of Isabelle or a branded visual.
Critical — No Twitter/X Card Tags (all 10 pages) twitter:card, twitter:title, twitter:description, twitter:image all absent. Unfurling on X will produce bare URLs.
Critical — No sitemap.xml The file does not exist at C:\Users\Rico\hummingbeing-website\sitemap.xml. Google has no machine-readable index of the site's pages. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console after creation. Should include all 10 pages with <lastmod> dates.
Critical — No robots.txt File does not exist. Without it, crawlers operate on defaults. Even a minimal file pointing to the sitemap is important. Also: changelog.html should arguably be noindex to avoid diluting crawl budget with a non-commercial page.
Critical — Title Tags are generic on 6/10 pages "About — HummingBeing", "Isabelle — HummingBeing", "Gallery — HummingBeing", "Social Media — HummingBeing" are weak. None of these secondary pages include a geographic signal (Singapore, Japan) or a service keyword in the title. The home page title "Release. Renew. Restore." is evocative but has zero keyword in it — a missed opportunity for the most-linked page.
Critical — Meta descriptions missing location on 9/10 pages Only book.html mentions "Singapore and Japan". For a local/regional service business where searches include "somatic coaching Singapore" or "TRE provider Tokyo", the location omission across all other pages is a significant ranking signal gap.
Warning — No meta keywords (all 10 pages) Google ignores meta keywords, but Bing and some other engines still reference them, and they signal intent during AEO/GEO content analysis. Low priority but worth a 10-minute addition.
Warning — No robots meta tag (all 10 pages) No explicit <meta name="robots" content="index,follow">. While default crawl behavior is "index, follow", being explicit avoids any ambiguity — especially when robots.txt is eventually added.
Warning — Favicon SVG only Every page uses <link rel="icon" href="favicon.svg">. No PNG fallback, no apple-touch-icon, no 32×32 / 16×16 ICO. Safari and some Android browsers may fail to display the favicon. Apple devices will show a blank icon when bookmarked to home screen.
Warning — Book form is non-functional The contact form on book.html has an onsubmit="handleBookSubmit(event)" that only hides the form and shows a thank-you message — no data is transmitted. There is no Formspree endpoint, no EmailJS, no fetch to a backend. Isabelle receives zero submissions from the website. This is both a business critical bug and a conversion tracking issue.
Positive — lang="en" on all 10 pages Every HTML tag has lang="en". Correct and consistent.
Positive — Meta descriptions present on all 10 pages Every page has a unique meta description. Quality varies but none are blank.
Positive — H1 present and unique on all 10 pages No duplicate H1s detected, no missing H1s.
Positive — Image alt text largely populated The vast majority of images have descriptive alt text. Several alts are generic (e.g. "Isabelle Claus Teixeira" with no context) but none are blank or "image.jpg".
Positive — Preload hints on hero images All pages use <link rel="preload" as="image"> for hero poster images, improving LCP performance.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Not Configured

AEO targets how Google's AI Overview, Google's featured snippets, Bing Answers, Perplexity, and similar engines extract and surface direct answers. The primary mechanic is structured data (JSON-LD) and well-formatted FAQ / how-to content.

Critical — Zero JSON-LD / Schema.org on any page This is the single largest gap in the entire site. No structured data exists anywhere. The content absolutely warrants and qualifies for:
  • Organization — on index.html: name, url, logo, contactPoint, sameAs (social profiles), address
  • Person — on isabelle.html: Isabelle's full bio, jobTitle, credentials, sameAs (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Amazon author)
  • Service — on tre.html, somatic-coaching.html, somatic-bodywork.html: serviceName, provider, areaServed, offers (price, priceCurrency)
  • FAQPage — on book.html (6 FAQ items exist), tre.html: ready-made FAQ content just needs schema wrapping
  • Event — on events.html: 3 TRE® Provider Certification modules with exact dates, prices, location (Nilayam, Singapore)
  • VideoObject — on gallery.html and tre.html: YouTube embed with title "TRE Star Groups with Isabelle Claus Teixeira" has no schema
  • PriceSpecification / Offer — on book.html and somatic-coaching.html: $2,200 Starter, custom Deep Dive
  • Book — on socials.html: "Magic Somatic" by Isabelle, linked to Amazon
Critical — FAQ content exists but is unstructured book.html has 6 FAQ items ("Do I need previous experience?", "Do I need to talk about my trauma?", "How many sessions will I need?", "Can sessions be done online?", "Is this available for corporate teams?", "What is the cancellation policy?") implemented as an accordion with JavaScript. The text is present but there is no FAQPage JSON-LD. Google and Bing cannot extract these as featured snippets or answer boxes.
Warning — No HowTo schema on TRE page tre.html has a 4-step process ("Prepare the Body", "Activate the Psoas", "Allow the Tremors", "Rest & Integrate") that is perfect for HowTo schema. This format is frequently surfaced in AI Overviews for "how does TRE work" type queries.
Warning — Definition-style content without schema somatic-bodywork.html has an explicit definition of "soma" (ancient Greek + Sanskrit etymology). This is ideal AEO content for "what is somatic bodywork" queries but has no DefinedTerm or speakable schema.
Positive — Rich, direct-answer-ready body text The content quality is excellent. Definitions are given in plain language, processes are step-by-step, benefits are listed, questions are anticipated. The content would answer AI Overviews well if the pages had proper schema and canonical signals to surface them.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

Partial

GEO targets how Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and similar LLMs represent the business in their generative outputs. Key factors: entity clarity, factual density, authoritative citations, structured content, and consistent NAP (Name/Address/Phone) signals.

Critical — No machine-readable entity signals The business has no JSON-LD Organization or Person schema, no sameAs array linking to social profiles, no structured NAP. LLMs that crawl the live site cannot confidently identify HummingBeing as a distinct entity separate from BHD Asia. The sameAs array on an Organization schema (linking to Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, bhdasia.com, traumaprevention.com) would dramatically improve entity confidence.
Critical — No Wikipedia / Wikidata presence Neither HummingBeing nor Isabelle Claus Teixeira appear to have Wikipedia or Wikidata entries. This is the strongest external entity signal for LLMs. While creating Wikipedia articles requires notability standards, a Wikidata entry for the business and person can be created freely and links back to the site as an "official website" reference.
Warning — Practitioner credentials scattered, not consolidated Isabelle's credentials (TRE® Certified Provider, ICF PCC, Strozzi Bodywork, Reiki Level II, 25+ years HR Fortune 100) are spread across isabelle.html and socials.html. A structured Person JSON-LD on isabelle.html with hasCredential, hasOccupation, alumniOf, memberOf would give LLMs a single authoritative source to cite from.
Warning — "Magic Somatic" book not prominently featured Isabelle has published a book listed on socials.html linking to Amazon. This is a significant authority signal for LLMs (published authors receive more generative citations). The book should be featured on isabelle.html with Book schema (name, author, isbn if available, url) and cross-linked from at least 3 other pages.
Warning — Third-party authority citations thin The site references Dr. David Berceli (TRE® founder), Dr. Stephen Porges (Polyvagal Theory), and Richard Strozzi-Heckler (Strozzi Institute) in body text. However, no outbound links to traumaprevention.com, Strozzi Institute, or published research exist from the service pages. Adding these links with proper context ("developed by...") strengthens the site's academic alignment — an important GEO signal.
Positive — Factual density is high The site is rich with specific, citable facts: 25+ years experience, 9 countries, 40+ nationalities, Dr. David Berceli, Dr. Stephen Porges' Polyvagal Theory, Strozzi Institute, Singapore and Japan locations, pricing ($2,200 Starter, 1,290–4,500 SGD certification). LLMs can cite these specifics if they can correctly attribute them to HummingBeing as an entity.
Positive — Chatbot knowledge base is excellent GEO content scripts.js contains a 30-entry FAQ knowledge base covering every key question about the business. This content is not visible to search crawlers or LLMs (it is inside a JS IIFE). If this content were replicated as visible HTML — perhaps as a well-structured FAQ page — it would be prime GEO content.
Positive — External links to authoritative organisations socials.html links to traumaprevention.com (TRE® Global), strozziinstitute.com, bhdasia.com, noomii.com/isabelle profile. These outbound links signal topical authority. The Amazon book link is also an external E-E-A-T signal.

Google Analytics & Tag Manager

Completely Missing
Critical — No analytics or tag manager on any page A thorough search of all 10 HTML files and scripts.js found zero occurrences of: gtag, GTM-, UA-, G-, _ga, analytics.js, ga.js, measurementId, dataLayer, googletagmanager.com, google-analytics.com. The website has been running with zero visitor data collection. You have no data on: which pages are visited, where users come from, how long they stay, what they click, whether they convert.
Critical — No conversion tracking on book.html book.html is the conversion page. The form submit fires a local JS handler that shows a thank-you message only. There is no conversion event, no goal completion, and the form does not send data anywhere. This is a double problem: (1) no analytics to measure conversions, and (2) no form backend to receive enquiries.
Critical — No Facebook Pixel or LinkedIn Insight Tag Given Isabelle is active on Instagram and LinkedIn, retargeting pixels from those platforms are completely absent. Visitors cannot be retargeted on those platforms.
Recommended Tracking Setup (in order):
  1. Create a GA4 property at analytics.google.com, get the Measurement ID (format: G-XXXXXXXXXX)
  2. Add the gtag snippet to every HTML page's <head> — or install GTM container and add gtag via GTM
  3. Fix the book.html form (add Formspree or EmailJS endpoint) and fire a GA4 conversion event on successful submission
  4. Create a Goal in GA4 for "form_submit" event from book.html
  5. Add Meta Pixel for Instagram retargeting
  6. Add LinkedIn Insight Tag for LinkedIn retargeting
  7. Verify Search Console property and submit sitemap
