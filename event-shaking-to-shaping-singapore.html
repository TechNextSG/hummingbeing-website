An ICF-accredited Introduction to TRE™ for Coaches

A 3-hour, in-person workshop in Singapore, accredited by the ICF: each workshop carries 3 ICF CCEs (1.5 Resource Development + 1.5 Core Competencies). Please note this in-person workshop does not include TRE™ personal sessions.

TRE™ (Tension, Stress & Trauma Releasing Exercises), developed by Dr. David Berceli, activates the body’s natural tremoring response: a neurogenic mechanism that discharges accumulated stress, tension and trauma from the nervous system, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation.

In this workshop you will explore how to use TRE™ within your coaching practice — as self-care, so you embody presence and body-led listening skills as a coach.

You can then continue your training with TRE™ Module 1 (21 ICF CCEs) and Modules 2 and 3 to become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider — accredited to offer TRE™ to individuals and groups.

What will be covered

  • A full TRE™ session experience, guided by Global TRE™ Certifying Trainers.
  • The neuroscience behind neurogenic tremoring and nervous system regulation.
  • How to integrate TRE™ into 1-on-1 and group coaching contexts.
  • Embodied presence and body-led listening skills as a coach.
