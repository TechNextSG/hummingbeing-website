What Is Somatic Healing?

Healing That Works With Your Body

Somatic Healing is an integrative approach that brings together a range of body-based and energy-based modalities — Reiki, Somatic Bodywork, Breathwork and EFT — to support deep healing at every level: physical, emotional, mental and spiritual.

Rather than applying a single technique, Isabelle draws from these practices to meet you where you are — combining them intuitively based on what your body and nervous system need in each session.

The Practices

What Each Modality Offers

Four complementary approaches — each powerful alone, transformative in combination.

Reiki

A gentle energy healing practice that works with the body’s energy field to release blockages, restore flow and promote deep relaxation. Isabelle is a certified Reiki Level II practitioner. Conducted fully clothed; hands may be placed on or hovering above the body.

Somatic Bodywork

Using the Strozzi method, gentle and systematic hands-on touch releases historical tension patterns held in the body. As the body opens, energy frees up and you find more choice in how you respond to life. Fully clothed.

Breathwork

Conscious breathing practices that activate the body’s natural healing response, regulate the nervous system and release stored emotional and physical tension. Breathwork can create profound shifts in states of anxiety, grief, stress or stuckness.

EFT — Emotional Freedom Technique

EFT (tapping) combines acupressure with focused awareness to release emotional blocks and recalibrate the nervous system’s stress response. Effective for anxiety, trauma responses, limiting beliefs and emotional reactivity. Simple to learn; powerful in practice.
