For Coaches

Why a Somatic Tool Belongs in Coaching

Coaching asks clients to think clearly, notice patterns and choose new actions. That is hard to do when the nervous system is stuck in stress. More and more coaches are adding body-based practices so that clients — and the coach — can settle before they think.

TRE™ is one of the simplest of these practices. It needs no equipment and no talking about the past, and once learned it becomes a self-care practice you can use for the rest of your life. Module 1 starts with you: your own regulation, your own presence in the room.

What coaches tell us they take away

  • A reliable way to come back to calm between demanding sessions.
  • Greater presence and steadier attention with clients.
  • First-hand understanding of how stress lives in the body.
  • A clear path to offering TRE™ to clients later, through Modules 2 and 3.

Credential Renewal

How the CCEs Count

ICF credentials (ACC, PCC and MCC) are renewed every three years with continuing coach education. CCEs are split into Core Competencies (directly about coaching skills) and Resource Development (knowledge and tools that support your coaching).

TRE™ Module 1 gives you 5 Core Competency and 16 Resource Development CCEs — a meaningful share of a renewal cycle from a single course. Keep your certificate of completion: you will need it when you log your hours with ICF.

Questions

Coaches’ FAQ

How many ICF CCEs does TRE™ Module 1 carry?

21 ICF Continuing Coach Education units: 16 in Resource Development and 5 in Core Competencies.

Do I have to be a coach to join?

No. Module 1 is open to anyone. The CCEs are a bonus for ICF-credentialed coaches who want them to count towards credential renewal.

After Module 1, can I use TRE™ with my coaching clients?

Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ for your own personal practice. To offer TRE™ to clients you complete Modules 2 and 3 and become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider.
