About this workshop

TRE™ (Tension, Stress & Trauma Releasing Exercises) activates the body’s natural tremoring response — a neurogenic mechanism that discharges accumulated stress, tension and trauma from the nervous system. In this workshop you will explore how to use TRE™ within your coaching practice — not just as a self-care tool, but as a somatic method that helps clients move from activation to regulated presence, from trembling uncertainty to purposeful, embodied action.

What you will explore

  • The neuroscience behind neurogenic tremoring and nervous system regulation
  • How to integrate TRE™ into 1-on-1 and group coaching contexts
  • Somatic self-care practices to prevent compassion fatigue and burnout
  • Embodied presence and body-led listening skills as a coach
  • A full TRE™ session experience guided by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer

Taught in English · in-person sessions also available in Romanian.
