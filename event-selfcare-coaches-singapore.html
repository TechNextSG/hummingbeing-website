Self-Care & Presence for Coaches

This 2-day in-person workshop is designed specifically for coaches — whether in training or at ACC, PCC or MCC level — who want to develop embodied self-care practices, prevent burnout and compassion fatigue, and deepen their somatic presence with clients.

TRE™ (Tension, Stress & Trauma Releasing Exercises) activates the body’s natural neurogenic tremoring response — a reflex that discharges stored stress and tension from the nervous system, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation. This workshop counts as Module 1 of the Global TRE™ Provider Certification Program.

Who it is for

  • Coaches in training (ACC, PCC & MCC level)
  • Those looking to prevent & recover from compassion fatigue and burnout
  • Anyone wanting a body-led modality for deep nervous system resilience
  • Coaches seeking to complement their certifications with TRE™
  • Those considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification (Modules 1, 2 & 3)

Programme — 2 days in Singapore

Day 129 Aug · Saturday · SGT

TRE™ Module 1 — Part 1

Introduction to TRE™ and the neuroscience of neurogenic tremoring. Guided full TRE™ session and somatic awareness practices. Personal resilience tools for coaches and helping professionals.

Day 230 Aug · Sunday · SGT

TRE™ Module 1 — Part 2 & Completion

Deepening the practice, integration and completion of Module 1 requirements. Guidance toward Modules 2 and 3 of the Global TRE™ Provider Certification. Certificate of completion issued.
